How the verdicts work

  • NEW LOW — the lowest price ever tracked for this product at this store.
  • STRONG DEAL — a real cut, but the product has been this cheap before, or the same price is available elsewhere.
  • ROUTINE SALE — a small cut or a rollback to an old price; worth knowing, not worth rushing for.

When the same product is listed at two stores, the detail view shows both prices side by side so you can see which store is actually cheaper. Prices change during Black Friday — always confirm on the store page before buying.
